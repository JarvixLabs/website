About Jarvix

Engineering-led consulting for cloud-native teams

Jarvix is a consultancy based in Pune, India. We help companies build the platforms, pipelines and reliability practices that let their engineering and data teams focus on the product.

Our mission

Make great engineering the default

Most teams know what good looks like: fast, safe deployments, reliable systems and AI that reaches production. What they lack is the time and focused expertise to build it. We bring both, and we leave the capability behind with your team.

What we believe

Our principles

Platforms are products

Internal platforms succeed when they're built around developer needs, with clear interfaces, documentation and feedback loops.

Automate the toil

Anything done twice by hand is a candidate for code. Automation frees engineers for the work that matters.

Reliability is a feature

Users notice outages before features. We make reliability explicit, measurable and part of everyday decisions.

Leave teams stronger

We pair, document and coach throughout, so success doesn't depend on us staying.
